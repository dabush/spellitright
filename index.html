Spell It RightTeacher page

Step 1Add your words

The app looks up each word's pronunciation, definition and an example sentence in a free online dictionary. Some words have no example sentence, so you may need to write one. You can change anything in step 2.

Step 2Check the details

This is an example list so you can see how it works. Select Clear list to start your own.

Step 3Share with students

The whole list is saved inside the link, so there are no accounts and nothing is stored anywhere. If you change the list, make a new link. When students finish, they copy a results summary and paste it wherever you collect work.

Check a student's results

Every results summary ends with a verification code. Paste a summary here to confirm it hasn't been edited. This works in the browser where you made the student link.
